AWS ECS migration

I led a four-person team through a monolith migration at Mypcot Infotech. We deployed four services independently.

MonolithAWS ECS4 services

How it worked
Problem
The client asked for the split. As the project grew, the monolith became hard to maintain, and a failure in one part could not be allowed to break the whole system.
My part
I worked with a third-party consultant to design the target architecture from the start, and led the migration. We built it around a shared database and the saga pattern.
Result
Independent deployments and better service isolation.
