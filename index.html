How AI Bestie works

One voice companion, two personalities. This is what happens between your question and its spoken reply.

  1. 1You speak or typeSpeech recognition captures your question. Typed and spoken messages share one conversation.
  2. 2The persona answersThe backend sends the last 20 messages as context and streams the reply over SSE as it forms.
  3. 3It speaks backFish Audio speaks the reply sentence by sentence, as each one arrives.
  4. 4You can cut inStart talking while it speaks and it stops to listen.

Two personalities. Friend and Mentor each have their own voice and range of settings. Five traits shape a reply, from how warmly the companion responds to how directly it challenges you.

How I ship a voice agent

From the mic to the metrics, in the order a turn travels. Open a step to see what breaks and what I build.

  1. 1CaptureMic in, speech to text
    What breaks
    Browser speech engines stop mid-word, and Safari behaves differently from Chrome. Words go missing without an error.
    What I build
    A capture loop that restarts the engine and keeps adding to the same transcript, with a server-side Whisper fallback for browsers that can’t transcribe.
  2. 2Turn detectionKnowing when someone has finished
    What breaks
    The agent jumps in on a short pause, or sits silent after the person is done.
    What I build
    Silence-based endpointing: 1.6 s of quiet ends the turn, stretched to 2.4 s when the sentence sounds unfinished.
  3. 3ReasoningA streamed reply, written for the ear
    What breaks
    A slow first token is dead air on a call, and long paragraphs sound robotic when spoken.
    What I build
    Token streaming over SSE, short replies in voice mode, recent conversation as context, and persona prompts that keep the tone steady.
  4. 4FailoverSurviving a slow or failing model
    What breaks
    One provider times out or hits a rate limit, and the whole call stalls.
    What I build
    A model list for each mode and hedged requests: if no token arrives in 2 s, the next model races alongside. No blind retries of the same model.
  5. 5SpeechText to speech, sentence by sentence
    What breaks
    Gaps between sentences, frozen playback, or a long wait for the first word.
    What I build
    Each sentence is spoken as soon as it is written, every synthesis call has a time limit, and the voice provider sits behind one switchable interface.
  6. 6Barge-inLetting people interrupt
    What breaks
    The agent keeps talking over the person. Nothing makes a voice agent feel fake faster.
    What I build
    The agent listens while it speaks. The first sign of the person’s voice stops playback and hands the turn back.
  7. 7TracingFollowing one turn end to end
    What breaks
    “It felt slow”, and no way to tell whether speech recognition, the model or the voice caused it.
    What I build
    One turn ID from the browser to the server. Mic, transcription, model, every audio chunk and playback land on one timeline.
  8. 8Metrics and logsMeasuring every turn
    What breaks
    No numbers, so no way to know whether a change made things faster or slower.
    What I build
    One JSON line per event: time to first token, stream time, total, provider, failovers and outcome. Timings only, never what anyone said.
  9. 9Load testingFinding the limit before users do
    What breaks
    A bottleneck nobody sees until real traffic arrives.
    What I build
    Load tests with mocked providers on one machine. The first run showed a TTS queue capping voice at about 10 users. With it raised, 100 concurrent voice users ran with 0 errors, and the server added 26 ms to a turn.
  10. 10ShippingSecured, tested, deployed
    What breaks
    Leaked keys, abuse, and regressions nobody notices until users do.
    What I build
    JWT auth, rate limits, secrets kept out of the code, tests on the voice paths, and a repeatable deploy.

Chatbots run on the same backbone. Text chat skips capture, turn detection, speech and barge-in. Reasoning, failover, tracing, metrics, load testing and shipping work the same way.
